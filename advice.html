All Advice Options

Choose the level of advice that suits your situation.

General Advice
General guidance without a personal recommendation

General advice can help you build your understanding of financial concepts through factual information and education, explore what financial planning involves, and stay informed as financial rules and regulations evolve.

General advice may be useful if you want to:

Learn more about a financial topic
Understand broad product features
Compare general types of options
Build your knowledge before seeking personal advice
Learn About General Advice
General advice (also referred to as general information or factual information) provides high level guidance only. It does not consider your personal objectives, financial situation or needs, and does not include product advice or recommendations.
One-off Personal Advice
Focused advice for a particular need

You may need advice about one issue without requiring a review of your entire financial position. One-off advice focuses on a clearly agreed area or a limited number of related matters. We will confirm what the advice covers, what information is required and the applicable fee before the work begins.

Subject to our authorisations, advice may cover areas such as:

Superannuation and SMSF
Retirement Planning
Investments
Personal Insurance
Cashflow and Debt Reduction
Discuss a Specific Advice Need
Comprehensive Personal Advice
Bringing the different pieces together

Comprehensive advice may be appropriate when several areas of your financial life need to be considered together. We begin by understanding your circumstances and priorities. After agreeing on the scope, we collect the relevant information, consider the available options and prepare our recommendations.

The process may include:

Agreeing on the scope of advice
Gathering relevant information
Understanding your objectives and priorities
Researching appropriate options
Preparing and presenting the advice
Assisting with implementation
Explore Comprehensive Advice
Ongoing Advice
Advice that continues as your circumstances change

This level supports clients who value ongoing guidance, regular reviews and strategic updates as their circumstances change. Ongoing advice is not automatically included and may not be suitable for everyone. We will explain what the service includes, how often it is provided and what it costs before you decide whether to proceed.

The ongoing service may include:

Portfolio monitoring
Annual review of your personal and financial situation
Annual portfolio performance and progress report
Administration and implementation support
Access to your adviser via phone and email
1–2 ad‑hoc in‑person meetings per year
Annual insurance needs optimisation
Coordination with accountant, finance broker or estate planner (if needed)
